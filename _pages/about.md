I'm Hanna (Hoa) Vo, currently a Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at the American University. 

My research interests are in applied microeconomics, at the intersection of labor, health, development, and demographic/household economics. My dissertation research has focused on intrahousehold decision-making and bargaining power dynamics, and its link to women's labor supply. Trained in quantitative economics and econometrics, causal inference methods for impact evaluation (including policy impact), machine learning and AI, data mining, and business intelligence. 
Previously, I worked for UN-ECLAC to produce a report on the U.S. new industrial policy (with focus on clean and renewable energy), and also worked as a Consultant in the private sector. 

I hold a bachelor's degree in Finance from University of Wisconsin, and earned my M.A. in Economics from Georgia State University (Andrew Young School of Policy Studies - AYSPS).
